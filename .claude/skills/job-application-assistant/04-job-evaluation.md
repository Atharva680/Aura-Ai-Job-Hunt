## Location Gate
- PASS: Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Mumbai, Nagpur, remote
- FLAG: other Indian metros with relocation support
- FAIL: no relocation/remote option outside above list

## Language Gate
Compare posting's required language against the Languages table in `01-candidate-profile.md`.
- Requires a language not listed at all → FAIL, hard stop.
- Requires a listed language at a higher bar than declared → FLAG, proceed, note the gap.
- Requires a listed language at/below declared level → PASS.

- <30: Poor Fit — skip
